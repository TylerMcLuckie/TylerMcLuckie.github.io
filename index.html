Welcome

I'm a creative designer, developer and user experience researcher. I aim to combine my understanding of the user with the beauty of design and the logical perfection of development. My interest in emerging technologies led me to complete a Bachelor of Creative Media Production specialising in Creative Technology and Game Development.

I continue to learn daily, pushing my limits to discover unique ways of creating engaging and holistic experiences for the user, applying principles of accessibility, inclusivity and ethics.
